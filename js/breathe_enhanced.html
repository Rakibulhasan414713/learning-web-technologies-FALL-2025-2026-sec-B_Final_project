ADHD Grounding Techniques

🔹 5-Min Task Attack – Set a 5‑minute timer and focus on one tiny task.
🔹 Object Focus – Describe an object in front of you in detail.
🔹 Physical Reset – Jump, stretch, or shake arms for 20 seconds.

PTSD Calming Exercises

🔹 Temperature Reset – Hold something cold to interrupt the stress spike.
🔹 Safe-Place Visualization – Imagine a calm place with sensory details.
🔹 Anchoring Phrase – Say: “I am safe right now.”

Anxiety Relief Techniques

🔹 5‑4‑3‑2‑1 Grounding – Use your senses to calm your nervous system.
🔹 Progressive Muscle Release – Tense muscles for 5s, release.
🔹 Slow Breathing – 4 seconds inhale, 6 seconds exhale.

Childhood Trauma Support

🔹 Inner Child Letter – Write supportive words to your younger self.
🔹 Comfort Objects – Hold a soft item or warm drink for body safety signals.
🔹 Re-parenting Affirmation – “I deserve safety, care, and understanding.”

Bullying Trauma Recovery

🔹 Identity Rebuild – List strengths bullies could NOT take from you.
🔹 Mirror Kindness Exercise – Speak one positive truth about yourself.
🔹 Safe People Check – Identify who supports you emotionally.

General Mental Health Practices

🔹 Journaling Release – Write without judging or editing.
🔹 Nature Break – Sit outside for 3 minutes and notice surroundings.
🔹 Digital Detox – 20 minutes with no screen to reset overstimulation.
